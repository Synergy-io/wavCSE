## What the two directions of a pair must mean

**DERIVED:** Define for a given pair and fixed target-specific protocol `T(B←A)=Y_B(A present)−Y_B(A absent)` and `T(A←B)` analogously. Differences are in *different target metrics* with different baselines and ceiling/head cardinality; a raw `T(B←A)−T(A←B)` in accuracy fractions is descriptively computable but **not** a calibrated cross-target effect size. A directional claim should prospectively name the target-specific meaningful gain, show positive paired evidence for one direction and absent/adverse controlled evidence for reverse at matched exposure, then compare signs or prespecified within-task-standardized deltas; do not choose a normalization from test data. Intervening on one edge of a **three-task** model while holding all other edges and training fixed is a separate test from removing an auxiliary dataset, which alters sampling and task mixture. Both probe different mechanisms.

**OBSERVED:** [DG-0002 five-seed gradient confirmation](../../improvements/taskrelation/research/studies/DG-0002/confirmation_result.json) gives late symmetric cosine KS--SID +.0022, KS--ER +.0013, SID--ER +.0221, with no persistent conflict in any seed. It can diagnose optimization alignment, **not** which member donates useful information. Source/target hypotheses based on speech semantics are explicitly [HYPOTHESIZED] in [ASYMMETRIC_RELATIONS.md](ASYMMETRIC_RELATIONS.md) §“Can KS/SID/ER relations be directional?”.

**Protocol guard:** [VARIANT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md](../../improvements/taskrelation/research/VARIANT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md) fixes all-25-layer representation, task mixture, epoch checkpoint and mandatory wavCSE/MTRL controls for variant arms. Single-/pair-task interventions change task count; if conducted as a distinct *diagnostic* they require pre-specified exposure-matched controls and cannot silently replace the frozen three-task benchmark. No intervention is authorized by this document. [DIRECTIONAL_PREDICTIONS.md](DIRECTIONAL_PREDICTIONS.md) separates a directed coefficient test from a causal transfer test.
