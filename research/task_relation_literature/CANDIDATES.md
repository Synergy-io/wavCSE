Not a surrogate for layer groups (layers already pooled). |

All six rows assuming coefficient relations require equal *feature-coordinate* dimension, **not** necessarily equal label spaces in abstract. Their published scalar/binary task coefficient-column layout cannot receive complete heads of shapes `(12,2000)`, `(1251,2000)`, `(4,2000)`. An average head row yields `(2000,3)` but loses class-specific effects and sends an identical summary-level regularizer gradient through all classes. A shared trunk is *one* parameter vector, not three independent aligned `w_t`; task gradients of it are another object, and using them changes the method. Task-conditioned equal-width adapters are architectural changes; frozen WavLM layers stay frozen only if those adapters are downstream.

## Shortlist and distinct hypotheses

1. **Existing dense corrected-MTRL vs existing precision p-MSSL** are *mandatory context*, not new literature inventions. `H_dense`: no conditional edge should be suppressed once normalization is corrected. `H_conditional`: one precision edge is persistently low and its shrinkage removes harmful *conditional* coupling. TR-0007 / DG-0007 already address these; **do not duplicate**. The latter is not established simply by reporting a zero edge.
2. **CANDIDATE-A SPATS — covariance sparsity, conditional gate.** `H_cov_sparse`: selectively setting marginal-covariance edges to zero helps while zeroing precision edges does not (or vice versa). Unlike TR-0007, the estimand itself changes; however a 3-task literature warning and summary inadequacy demand a prospective edge/utility check. At present a *future hypothesis only*, not a publication-backed claim of applicability to our neural heads.
3. **Existing TR-0008 directed question — not a new arm.** `H_directed`: matched source→target benefit differs materially from reverse direction, beyond optimizer exposure. Current observations weaken it. AMTL is lineage, TR-0008 is project-original; no AMTL attribution for its output.
4. **CANDIDATE-B MTHOL — higher-order mediated relation, gated.** `H_mediated`: third-task mediation predicts useful transfer beyond pairwise geometry. Before any experiment, require an *observed* nonsingular summary Gram and repeatable mediated term independent of the dominant rank-one component; otherwise it merely repackages MTRL's saturation.
5. **Dynamic/phase-specific relation** is a *diagnostic lead*, not an eligible published, parameter-based next arm here: time-varying Ω histories already exist, but no phase-specific gain or sign-conditioned utility is measured; AutoTR's “temporal” means tasks indexed by time, **not** training epoch. `H_dynamic` remains unlicensed until distinct primary method and phase-specific evidence. [OPEN_QUESTIONS.md](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md).

**Not shortlisted:** task grouping (Jacob, GO-MTL) is an adjacent formal clustering/low-rank branch; fixed graph Laplacian with supplied edges does not *learn* task relations; cross-stitch/selective routing are feature-sharing rather than a within-category parameter relation; GradNorm, uncertainty balancing, PCGrad and CAGrad do not learn the required relation object; MTGP and residual MSSL require different output likelihood/alignment; MTHOL is gated rather than endorsed.
