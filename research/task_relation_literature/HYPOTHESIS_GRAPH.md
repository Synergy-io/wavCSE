## Traversal rule

```
object defined and source/protocol qualified
    → stable statistic under seeds/folds (not extra task pairs)
    → target-specific prediction declared before reading target outcome
    → matched intervention preserving representation and optimizer opportunity
    → per-task result, paired seeds, ER LOSO where required
    → retained or falsified *within that protocol*
```

This graph cannot be traversed from **a symmetric cosine** to a directed claim, from **precision zero** to “no task transfer,” or from **one seed** to a confirmed improvement. Three undirected or six directed edges are fixed, mutually dependent comparisons: cross-edge `n=3` correlations do not estimate a population relation-outcome law ([IDENTIFIABILITY.md](IDENTIFIABILITY.md)). The single largest common prerequisite is validation that class-mean heterogeneous-head relation objects track *controlled utility* at all.
