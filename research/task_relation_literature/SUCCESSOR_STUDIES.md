## CANDIDATE-A — sparse covariance versus sparse precision

1. **Question:** When only three tasks exist, do zeros in *marginal task-head covariance* have different transfer consequences than zeros in *conditional precision*?
2. **Hypothesis / competitor:** `H_cov_sparse`: one stable task-pair covariance edge should be suppressed; this preserves useful other edges and improves at least the harmed task. Competitor: all edges are useful/near-unidentifiable, or p-MSSL's *conditional* edge notion—not marginal covariance—is the relevant one; head summary may cause either pattern.
3. **Paper:** [Zhang & Yang 2017 SPATS](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/10820/10679), Eq. (3), Theorem 1; [p-MSSL 2016](https://jmlr.org/papers/volume17/15-215/15-215.pdf), Eq. (3),(8) is an *existing contrasting estimand*, not the proposed implementation.
4. **Status / learned object:** `IN_SCOPE_WITH_JUSTIFIED_ADAPTATION`: original shallow aligned per-task coefficient columns become the *same* 2000-dimensional class-mean head summaries used by MTRL; the learned 3×3 `C⪰0` is ℓ₁-penalized **covariance**, not precision, not head class correlations. A change of loss from linear/binary to neural multiclass and alternating gradients needs an explicit attribution limit. No alteration to frozen upstream embedding or 25L policy.
5. **Expected behavior:** If SI--ER is truly harmful under matched controls, its covariance edge may shrink while KS--SID/KS--ER persist. That pair assignment is a **hypothesis** from approximate DG-0001 evidence, not a known optimal zero. Shared trunk still exchanges updates even when `C_SI,ER=0`.
6. **Falsification:** Across matched seeds/folds the proposed zero-edge pattern fails to recur, the model simply collapses to diagonal or saturated/uninformative relation, or validation-selected candidate does not improve paired task results versus **both** controls without a >0.20 percentage-point regression on any task; LOSO does not resolve ER benefit. A performance benefit without distinct covariance-vs-precision structure tests only a generic regularization change, not `H_cov_sparse`.
7. **Minimum test:** Before paid compute, rank/variance diagnostic of existing 25L head summaries and pre-specified predicted pair pattern; future approved one-seed screen against matched controls, then gated five-seed confirmation. Do not run an opportunistic λ sweep. SPATS §Experiments selected λ on validation over a grid, but at `m=3` there is no transferable optimum; a *small predefined validation-only matched-budget* grid needs prospective study registration and human signoff, all attempted settings recorded before confirmation.
8. **Controls/comparators:** wavCSE, historical MTRL matched, normalization-corrected MTRL if current comparator changes under DG-0007, p-MSSL TR-0007 only after its exact arm is settled; hold summary normalization constant or make it the explicitly controlled independent variable. Baseline p-MSSL is NOT simply 'SPATS with λ=0': precision vs covariance remain different objects.
9. **LOSO / diagnostics:** Yes for any ER claim; actual per-epoch covariance and inverse/partial correlation separately, eigenvalues, off-diagonal support, seeds/folds, rank of aligned summaries, head-specific versus shared gradients, batch composition and validation trajectory. Distinguish suppression of a *summary penalty* from reduced shared-gradient interference.
10. **Implementation difficulty / compute:** Moderate covariance PSD proximal update, low 3×3 matrix cost; full deep training and LOSO dominate. Qualitatively one new candidate arm + matched controls for a screen; multi-seed/LOSO only after screening and separate authorization. Existing [DG-0002](../../improvements/taskrelation/research/studies/DG-0002/analysis.md) records ~18–19 minutes for a **different** single-seed setup, insufficient to quote a general future cost.

## CANDIDATE-B — mediated higher-order structure (conditional on rank diagnostic)

1. **Question:** Does a third task reveal *useful mediated parameter similarity* beyond pairwise covariance/precision, or merely amplify the dominant near-rank-one summary direction?
2. **Hypothesis / competitor:** `H_mediated`: for target/source pair `i,j`, the **third-task term** `G_ik G_kj` in `(G²)_ij` carries stable additional information relevant to task-wise validation/LOSO. Competitor: `(G²)` is algebraically determined by existing `G` and mainly magnifies its leading eigenvalue; no new identifiable transfer statistic at three tasks.
3. **Paper:** [Zhang & Yeung 2013 MTHOL](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/13/Papers/283.pdf), §2.1–2.4 Eqs. (3),(8)–(10), `t=2` vs `t=1`.
4. **Status / learned object:** `IN_SCOPE_WITH_JUSTIFIED_ADAPTATION`, **currently not ready**. Original aligned real/binary coefficient `W` and Gaussian outcome likelihood become unequal multiclass head summaries / neural cross-entropy. Need show full-rank Gram and specify mathematically faithful replacement for Eq. (8)'s likelihood and determinant; otherwise a project-original high-order penalty, not MTHOL. Learned symmetric `C=(G²)/d` and MAP coefficient dependence, **not** a signed three-way causal interaction or directed edge.
5. **Expected behavior:** Third-task-mediated signal should remain distinct from the direct Gram term under seed/fold variation and should prospectively distinguish a validation improvement not explained by first-order relation strength.
6. **Falsification:** `rank(G)<3`, near-singular determinant requiring arbitrary jitter, mediated term unstable or fully dominated by rank-one factor, or valid matched confirmation gives no additional gain vs MTRL and wavCSE/LOSO. If no separation, do not pay for a high-order architecture.
7. **Minimum test:** Read-only diagnostic on existing (no new training) summary Gram eigenvalues and `(G²)_ij` decomposition on several 25L seeds/folds; require prospective rank and mediated-signal criterion **before** considering implementation. Conditional future screen only if a loss-consistent published mapping survives. Compare `t=1` vs `t=2` with fixed protocol; one degree test, no broad order sweep.
8. **Controls/comparators:** wavCSE and classical MTRL; DG-0007 corrected MTRL if the valid matched control; p-MSSL only if separating conditional versus mediated is part of a pre-stated primary question. Do not use test results to select `t`.
9. **LOSO / diagnostics:** Yes for ER performance claim; per-seed Gram singular values/condition number, mediated-vs-self terms, eigenvector stability, validation transfer residual, observed vs predicted pair ranking, exposure gates.
10. **Difficulty / compute:** High scientific/engineering port because paper's log determinant and Gaussian likelihood are not the current neural model; 3×3 algebra cheap but candidate/control deep runs costly. No number justified by repository timings.

## CANDIDATE-C — phase-dependent relation (diagnostic-only placeholder, **not a mechanism study**)

1. **Question/hypothesis:** Does a phase-specific measured relation *predict* matched later transfer better than a global relation? `H_dynamic` competes with ordinary convergence of a single static relation estimator.
2. **Primary method / fidelity:** **None verified for a three-task, non-temporal, deep parameter-relation mechanism here.** AutoTR 2023 is time-indexed *across tasks*, not training-phase-dependent. This is `PRIMARY_SOURCE_NOT_VERIFIED` for the proposed mechanism; **do not implement or register** as published-method successor.
3. **Object / expectations:** Prospective summaries of historical per-epoch `Ω`, phase-aligned controlled transfer/validation, matched data mixture; one can *observe* dynamic statistics without learning a new 3×3 parameter matrix. Existing LOSO Ω trajectories show KS--SID rises while ER edges spread ([synthesis §§Dynamic behavior](../../improvements/taskrelation/research/task_relations/MTRL_DIAGNOSTIC_SYNTHESIS.md)); do not infer that reweighting helps.
4. **Falsification/minimum:** Require temporally stable **out-of-seed** prospective prediction after controlling optimizer exposure, checkpoint policy and representation; absence refutes rationale for a mechanism. Existing diagnostic artifacts are an initial read-only starting point; a fresh intervention is separate, explicitly authorized.
5. **Controls / LOSO / diagnostics / cost:** Compare predictions against static per-epoch/global MTRL on the same seeds/folds; LOSO mandatory if predicting ER transfer; record time, sign, magnitude, conditioning and effective task batch counts. Diagnostic analysis low incremental compute if already stored; training requires separately scoped study. No architecture complexity estimated without verified method.
