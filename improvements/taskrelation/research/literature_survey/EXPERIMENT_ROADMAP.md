## NOT IDENTIFIABLE WITH CURRENT 3-TASK DESIGN / do not run yet

- **CANDIDATE-B (MTHOL high-order): `NEEDS_DIAGNOSTIC_FIRST`.** Before registering, ask whether 25L mean-head Gram is nonsingular with stable eigenvalues across *existing* seeds/folds and whether its mediated third-task term `G_{i,k}G_{k,j}` is distinguishable from `(G_{ii}+G_{jj})G_{ij}`. This is a **read-only arithmetic diagnostic** if parameter-summary histories exist; current Ω-only JSON does **not** contain all `W`/Gram trajectories. If rank-deficient/unavailable, do **not** invent jitter or a Gaussian-to-multiclass likelihood as if it were faithful MTHOL. A six-edge training intervention would still be needed for higher-order *utility*, not inferable from three algebraically dependent Gram terms. [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md), [SUCCESSOR_STUDIES.md](SUCCESSOR_STUDIES.md).
- **CANDIDATE-A status: `NEEDS_EXISTING_STUDY_RESULTS`.** SPATS primary warns sparsity may lose with four tasks. Until M1/M2 show an estimand difference and M3 a repeatable pair utility mismatch, registering it would duplicate “try sparsity” rather than distinguish covariance vs conditional precision. [SPATS primary Eq.(3), Table 2](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/download/10820/10679).
- **Grouped relation: `REJECT_FOR_NOW`** as a mechanism: three pair+singleton partitions are descriptive hypotheses, but task clustering/GO-MTL belongs to another research branch; no replicated useful subgroup is observed. A three-task partition and a two-edge conditional precision chain are **not equivalent** ([THREE_TASK_ANALYSIS.md](THREE_TASK_ANALYSIS.md) §2).
- **Dynamic mechanism: `NEEDS_DIAGNOSTIC_FIRST`, not a registered arm.** Existing Ω changes over LOSO epochs; no phase-conditioned target outcome benefit is measured. AutoTR's time-indexed *tasks* are not an epoch-dependent relation updater ([ASYMMETRIC_RELATIONS.md](ASYMMETRIC_RELATIONS.md)).
- Do **not** start a broad λ sweep, architecture zoo, new pooling/layer configuration or gradient-surgery arm. TR-0007 fixed λ₂=.01 for its screen; ER ordinary split cannot select a method; DG-0002 found no persistent gradient conflict; DG-0005 made gradient-scale task-intrinsic claims untenable. Do not compare unpaired historical seed-0 MSSL, prior MTRL `opt`, new `epoch`, or unmatched 16L/25L results as mechanism effects.

**Largest present uncertainty:** Whether a learned relation over class-mean heterogeneous heads represents useful *controlled transfer* at all. Normalization/precision/direction are three different representations, but none has yet shown a replicated, exposure-matched relation→target-benefit alignment. The summary adapter and shared-trunk confound can survive a clean three-way benchmark. The roadmap first reads existing studies, then asks for **one** discriminator rather than another architecture.
