# TR-0008: directed-object predictions versus useful directional transfer

**Status:** theory only. [DEC-0014](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/DECISIONS.md) names `TR-0008` as a **project-original** directed head-summary mechanism; this checkout's [STATE.md](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/STATE.md) says it is **not registered** and the exact rule is not set. The published aligned-vector methods [AMTL](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/leeb16.pdf) and the primary-verified [GAMTL](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/2019/0444.pdf) are *motivation*, not faithful algorithms over 12/1251/4-way wavCSE heads. Do not prescribe an equation for an unregistered TR-0008 or change its protocol. [ASYMMETRIC_RELATIONS.md](ASYMMETRIC_RELATIONS.md), [DIRECTIONAL_EVIDENCE.md](DIRECTIONAL_EVIDENCE.md), [VARIANT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/VARIANT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md).

## Three increasingly demanding claims

1. **Asymmetric *representation* (`OBSERVED` only when recorded):** `B_{A,B}≠B_{B,A}` for learned class-mean-summary coefficients, with a fixed convention `B_{source,target}` and zero diagonal. Report coefficients, their per-epoch histories, support, scale, variance across seeds and LOSO folds, loss factors, and all three task-summary Grams. A nonnegative coefficient denotes *positive reconstruction weight*; an absent edge is not a negative transfer observation.
2. **Asymmetric *regularization* (`DERIVED`):** if reconstruction penalty is `‖W−WB‖²_F`, its effective coupling is `Q=(I−B)(I−B)^T`, always symmetric PSD. `B` and `Q` are different objects; even a strongly unequal `B_{AB}`/`B_{BA}` does not mean the effect of the shared regularizer on task accuracy is directed. The actual TR-0008 rule is not yet specified, so this is a **conditional** derivation for AMTL-like penalties, not a claim about implemented TR-0008.
3. **Useful directional transfer (`HYPOTHESIZED`):** prospectively controlled `T(B←A)` is beneficial on B's target metric and matched reverse `T(A←B)` is absent, weaker under a *predefined target-standardized scale*, or adverse; signs/paired confidence and target-wise gains must replicate, with ER judged by LOSO. A coefficient alone satisfies only claim 1.

**Why not fit a correlation of edge value versus outcome across pairs?** Three undirected or six directed edges have shared tasks, shared trunk and the same optimizer; even five seeds repeat those *same edges*. n=3 cross-pair Pearson/Spearman claims are not inferential samples; pooling fold×seed×pair as independent observations is pseudoreplication. Appropriate checks: *predeclare a discrete pair prediction*, show per-seed sign/support stability, run a matched intervention for that pair, report all three task outcomes and held-out-speaker distribution. A leave-one-pair-out prediction has only two training edges and cannot claim general law. [IDENTIFIABILITY.md](IDENTIFIABILITY.md).

## Minimum conceptual interventions (not an experiment launch)

**I. Same three-task relation-edge intervention.** With frozen all-25-layer input and unchanged three-task sampler, intervene on one **incoming directed** coefficient or its learned availability (e.g., clamp edge A→B and compare to a matched sham constrained edge) while holding the other task weights, parameter budget, optimizer-exposure and checkpoint as comparably as the eventually registered implementation allows. Refit from paired seed; do not clamp only *after training* to claim training transfer. Changing B's task loss or the number of tasks would confound it. Which constraints are mathematically valid must await the **actual registered TR-0008 objective**; no invented architecture rule here.

**II. Independent pairwise transfer diagnostic.** For a B target, compare B-alone/no-A-influence vs B with A, then A-alone/no-B vs A with B, with target-specific control runs matching update count, effective per-task batch size, loss scaling, scheduler, shared-parameter budget and seed. DG-0001 matched *steps approximately* by changing batch size, which also changed noise; that is an informative negative diagnostic but not perfect exposure equality. Pre-register one pair and target-defined metric; use validation for selection, never test results. Pairwise task removal is a *different* training regime from the three-task benchmark and cannot replace its baseline/MTRL controls.

**ER:** A serious ER target effect is paired speaker-independent ten-fold LOSO, including validation speakers and fixed fold budget; folds are clustered within seed, not ten independent task pairs. For SID or KS targets tested with ER as auxiliary, prevent a leaky ER-task training split from silently supplying speaker identity as a shortcut; report its speaker split and also assess ER as a separate LOSO target before claiming improved KS/SID/ER joint transfer. Historical DG-0001 reverse fold intervals reuse one target-only reference. A valid directional confirmation needs *new* target-only references paired by seed/fold.

## Falsifiable outcomes, without claiming a winner

- `H_direction`: if A→B weight is stable and materially larger than B→A, beneficial *B target* control persists across seeds, reverse does not, and B benefit survives applicable LOSO, coefficient asymmetry has corresponding outcome evidence. **Falsify** the *utility* claim with no replicated positive B target delta, reverse benefits comparably, or loss/exposure-only control reproduces B gain. Those results do not logically prove covariance/precision methods optimal.
- `H_reconstruction_only`: stable asymmetric coefficients predict task-parameter reconstruction but no distinct target outcome. **Falsify** with prespecified, repeatedly matched one-way outcome gains specifically removed by the edge intervention and not by sham removal. This tests a mechanistic effect of the edge, not just that an auxiliary dataset helps.
- `H_exposure`: apparent direction results from unequal steps/composition (DG-0001's established failure). **Falsify** with one-way gains that survive matched step+batch+parameter controls; ordinary ER split alone cannot do so.

Current **OBSERVED** evidence ([EMPIRICAL_SYNTHESIS.md](EMPIRICAL_SYNTHESIS.md)): raw ER←KS +.3411 LOSO drops to +.0053 (CI includes zero), raw ER←SID +.2794 becomes −.0589 under approximate step matching; reverse KS←ER −.0038 and SID←ER −.0231 reuse single target-only references. No supported useful one-way pair exists. Gradient cosine near zero late in DG-0002 is symmetric and cannot change that verdict. `TR-0008` is a *question* already named, not an answer or a reproduction of AMTL.
