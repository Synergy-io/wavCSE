## Exact implemented contrast, not a claim of faithful Zhang–Yeung replication

**OBSERVED** [model lines 168–261](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/01-mtrl/mtrl_model.py): classifier of task `t` yields `w_t=[mean_c(weight_t[c,:]); mean_c(bias_t[c])]`, a 2001-dimensional vector (2000 hidden units plus bias); `W∈R^{3×2001}`. The flag `normalize_w` is applied inside **both** `update_omega()` and `get_mtrl_regularizer_loss()`:

\[
\widetilde w_t=\begin{cases}w_t/(\|w_t\|_2+\epsilon),&\texttt{normalize_w=true},\\w_t,&\texttt{normalize_w=false},\end{cases}\qquad
C(\widetilde W)={\sqrt{\widetilde W\widetilde W^\top}\over\operatorname{tr}\sqrt{\widetilde W\widetilde W^\top}},\qquad
R=\lambda\operatorname{tr}\bigl(\widetilde W^\top(C+\epsilon I)^{-1}\widetilde W\bigr).
\]

Here `epsilon=1e−4` in the [benchmark MTRL control](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/VARIANT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md) §2, λ=`mtrl_lambda=0.01`; Ω/`omega_inv` are buffers, refreshed analytically after warmup once per epoch, not optimized by backprop. **DERIVED:** removing row normalization changes **both** the Gram used by the analytic covariance update *and* the relation penalty's scale and head gradients. It is not a pure re-labeling of Ω. At exact collinearity with positive vectors, unit-normalized rows make `C≈11ᵀ/3`, an almost rank-one uniform structure; unnormalized row lengths allow diagonal/edge distinctions. Conversely, changing Ω may worsen conditioning or simply alter overall regularization without yielding useful transfer.

**D3: not fully faithful even without row normalization.** The [original paper Eq. (7)](https://event.cwi.nl/uai2010/papers/UAI2010_0144.pdf) penalizes its *whole aligned per-task parameter matrix* with **both** `(λ₁/2)‖W‖²_F` and `(λ₂/2)tr(W C^{-1}Wᵀ)` for a shallow linear/binary prediction model. wavCSE substitutes mean-of-unequal-class-head rows for those whole vectors and a generic network-wide l1/l2 regularization ([benchmark](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/VARIANT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md) §1), rather than the paper's separately specified `(λ₁/2)‖W‖²_F` on the *same* W; this is **D3**. It also uses a deep shared trunk, ε in the inverse, warmup and epoch-wise update. Removing row normalization addresses **one** deviation; it does not reproduce the paper's task-parameter model, its full objective or its convexity guarantee. No DG-0007 outcome can independently attribute effects to D3 or claim to evaluate unmodified Zhang–Yeung MTRL.

## Predictions, disconfirmations and interpretations

| Label | Prospective outcome, **HYPOTHESIZED** | Test / falsifier | What it would and would not mean |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| N1, geometry changes | `C_raw` differs from historical `C_unit` in diagonal distribution, eigenvalue ratio, pair sign/support or stability, beyond normal seed variability. | Paired seeds/folds and epoch-matched Ω histories with both raw `W` norms and normalized Gram; **falsify** if no repeatable meaningful difference. | Normalization changed *estimation geometry*, not automatically useful transfer. |
| N2, geometry changes but accuracy does not | Relation matrix/regularizer magnitude changes, yet matched per-task validation and paired fixed-epoch test deltas vs historical MTRL and wavCSE have no resolved material movement; LOSO unchanged within uncertainty. | Matched screen then approved seed0–4 confirmation under identical frozen 25L representation, steps and epoch checkpoint; LOSO for ER. **Falsify** with replicated outcome gain/regression attributable after matching. | Shows the normalization issue mattered **mathematically** but not detectably for generalization under this protocol; not exact equivalence, not a statement about the original paper. |
| N3, covariance predicts useful transfer | A prospectively specified pair's corrected covariance (magnitude/sign/stability, conditioned on raw head norms) predicts beneficial **controlled** target-specific transfer better than saturated historical Ω. | Independently collect exposure-matched interventions by seed for that pair, with ER LOSO; **falsify** if corrected association is stable and strong while controlled outcomes stay neutral/adverse. | Without intervention, prediction is an unvalidated utility interpretation of parameter geometry. |
| N4, normalization changes performance | Correction improves or harms accuracy because of a changed Ω *and/or* changed effective penalty gradient/scheduler path. | Record penalty norm, per-task head gradient scale, diagonal/eigenvalues, validation learning-rate trajectory alongside outcomes; if all these change together, causal attribution to Ω alone is **not identified**. | A confirmed benefit is a benefit of a *normalization-corrected adaptation*, not proof dense covariance is the correct relation object. |
| N5, proxy undermined | Even a differentiated corrected `C` is stable but misaligns with controlled beneficial pair-transfer signs across seeds/folds, or changes dramatically with representation while transfer does not. | Prospectively fixed pairwise utility readout against exposure-matched control; compare per-pair descriptive signs (not n=3 correlation). | Undermines **head-parameter covariance as a proxy for useful transfer**, not the mathematical validity of `C` as a covariance estimate. |

## Minimal reporting and valid comparisons

The hypothetical comparison must preserve [protocol](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/VARIANT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md) §§1–5: all 25 frozen layers, `smp(0.5)`, same train split/mixture/optimizer steps, 30 epochs, global batch 2048, epoch checkpoint and matched seeds; only `normalize_w` varies for this diagnostic. Compare historical normalized MTRL and correction **at the same commit and seed** with baseline as reference; use validation for any decisions, no test-based selection. Per epoch emit raw three head norms, raw Gram, `C` values/diagonals/eigenvalues/condition, penalty and head-gradient magnitude, actual validation/lr trajectory, per-task plus aggregate outcomes. For performance, one seed screens; seeds0–4 supply paired confidence/sign counts, and any serious ER claim requires held-out-speaker LOSO. An `Ω` change *without* outcome change is a legitimate scientific result. [DG-0001 controls](EMPIRICAL_SYNTHESIS.md) show saturated Ω need not mean useful transfer; [DG-0002](EMPIRICAL_SYNTHESIS.md) late symmetric cosine says nothing about direction.
