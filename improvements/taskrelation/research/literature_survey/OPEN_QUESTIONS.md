## Theoretical / source questions

1. **What are independent samples for a learned task relation?** In p-MSSL, `d` rows of task-parameter coefficients play the Gaussian graphical model's sample role. The class-mean head summary has ~2000 *coordinates*, not 2000 independent speech tasks or independent draws: feature coordinates are correlated by the shared hidden layer. Effective sample size, sparsity support uncertainty and validity of a Gaussian-row prior need empirical checking. Numerical invertibility of a 3×3 matrix is **not** statistical identifiability of its zeros.
2. **Does normalizing head-summary columns destroy variance?** Classical MTRL's unit normalization can create rank-near-one ±1/3 covariance; the behavior of corrected DG-0007 must be recovered from its *own* record when ready. Preserve exact matched summary/normalization between estimators unless explicitly studying normalization.
3. **Does the deep regularizer imply the shallow representer theorem?** SPATS Theorem 1 proves a no-direct-source-span property for its shallow optimum. In wavCSE, a shared trunk is updated by all task losses even with a zero head-relation edge; transferring the paper's total no-transfer claim to the network would be false.
4. **Does a directed coefficient have a unique causal meaning?** `w_t≈Σ_s B_st w_s` is an asymmetric representation of *aligned parameter vectors*. Near-collinear vectors can produce nonunique `B`, while `tr[W(I−B)(I−B)^T W^T]` is a symmetric effective penalty. Only matched interventions identify *performance* transfer direction.
5. **What published method, if any, updates a true parameter-based relation with training phase rather than a time index over tasks?** AutoTR's `temporal` refers to longitudinal task identities, not moving training epochs. No such ready-for-wavCSE source is verified here; avoid calling an invented scheduler a faithful published method.
6. **Can class-mean heads represent task-specific parameter dependence at all?** Averaging a 1251-class SI head and a 4-class ER head may wash out discriminative directions and imposes the same relation gradient on every class. Equal-size downstream task-conditioned adapters or common projections would be **new architectures**, require head-specific ablations and risk changing the benchmark's sole independent variable.
7. **Can a higher-order Wishart relation stay well-defined at near-rank-one `W`?** MTHOL's Eq. (8) contains `log|WᵀW|`. Adding numerical jitter is an estimator change; see [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md). Need positive smallest eigenvalue across actual seeds, not just `d≥m` by dimension.
8. **Can a signed precision entry represent negative transfer?** Partial correlation sign is opposite precision off-diagonal sign, conditional on a Gaussian coefficient model. Neither sign measures the causal sign of transfer. Sparse covariance `C_ij=0` and sparse precision `P_ij=0` have different meanings for the KS/SID/ER triangle.

## Empirical unknowns with discriminating measurements

1. **Which pair, if any, is *beneficial* after full exposure matching?** DG-0001: SI/ER appears harmful both directions after approximate step controls; KS/ER residual +0.0053 has CI [−0.0289,+0.0394]; KS/SID only one-seed screen. Match optimizer steps, per-task batch sizes, loss weights and training/checkpoint budgets under multiple seeds, then LOSO for ER. A symmetric cosine cannot answer this.
2. **Is any directed difference reproducible?** Estimate `T(B←A)−T(A←B)` per target on a commensurate normalized metric (accuracy floors/ceilings and class counts differ), with matched optimizer exposure *for each direction*, paired seeds and ER LOSO folds. Positive one way and null/negative reverse over independent seeds would be compelling; both near zero or similar sign/magnitude falsifies a useful directional mechanism. This is distinct from raw DG-0001 asymmetry.
3. **Does a relation-zero intervention help rather than merely correlate?** Compare matched conditional/marginal edge ablations with all else fixed, retain shared trunk, record validation performance and fold/seed relation stability; report if a sparse solver changes diagonal scaling or head loss optimization at the same time. A relation heat map by itself is not a causal claim.
4. **Does gradient near-orthogonality persist under LOSO or varied layer-specific diagnostics?** DG-0002 baseline late means +.002/+.001/+.022, negative frequencies .464/.426/.357 across five seeds, no persistent conflict under the *25L standard composition*. Do not retrofit a conflict-only gradient mechanism without new pre-specified evidence.
5. **Are ER-edge fluctuations estimator noise or genuine sample/speaker dependence?** Re-evaluate Ω under matched-composition controls if authorized; DG-0005 changes both sampled share and ER learning progress. Need conditional variance across independent draws and held-out-speaker axes, not just one training trajectory.
6. **Do high-order mediated terms add signal independently of the first Gram eigenvector?** Test out-of-seed stability and prospective validation/LOSO prediction; if the Gram is rank-near-one, powers amplify existing geometry without identifying a new relation.
7. **Does an observed relation change *predict* subsequent validation transfer?** Ω trajectories (25L LOSO KS--SID +.035→+.286; ER edges spread) are descriptive. A held-out-seed/fold prospective prediction under a fixed sampling/checkpoint protocol is needed before dynamic adaptation is a scientific mechanism candidate.
8. **What is the latest actual status of DG-0007 / TR-0007 / TR-0008?** This independent worktree starts at base `664c572...`; concurrent agents own those records in different worktrees. Current evidence is only this checkout's [STUDIES.jsonl](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/STUDIES.jsonl) and [DECISIONS](../../../../improvements/taskrelation/research/DECISIONS.md). Reconcile *after integration*, do not silently treat a parallel agent's intention as completed data.
